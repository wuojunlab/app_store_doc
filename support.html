Voice

How do I say a sum?

Hold the 🎤 button, say the sum (for example "twelve times thirty-five"), then let go. BVCalculator writes it out as 12 × 35 = and answers 420. A quick tap isn't enough: keep holding while you speak.

🎤 doesn't work or says "Equation not recognized"
  1. Check that BVCalculator may use the microphone and speech recognition: iOS Settings → BVCalculator, or Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone and Speech Recognition.
  2. Speak after you start holding 🎤 and let go when you finish.
  3. Say a whole sum, such as "five plus three". Times and divide come first, so "two plus three times four" is 14.
  4. Speech recognition may need an internet connection.
I can't hear the voice

Turn up the volume and check that the ring/silent switch or Action button isn't set to silent. If you use Bluetooth headphones, check they're connected. Tap 📢 to hear the number on screen again.

Which languages can it speak?

English, 中文, Español, Français, 日本語 and 한국어, for both voice in and voice out. English is included for everyone; the other five come with Unlock Everything. Change it in ☰ → Settings → Voice language.

Puzzles

How do points and stars work?

The faster you solve a puzzle, the more points it earns: up to 100 on Easy, 500 on Medium and 1,000 on Hard. Solve it within 5, 10 or 15 seconds for full points; after that the points fall evenly to a tenth by 1, 1½ or 2 minutes. A second try or a hint halves them. Each solved puzzle also earns 1 to 3 stars. A puzzle that's wrong twice, skipped or left after a wrong answer earns 0. Each level's stars are your average per puzzle played. Open 🏅 Best scores → "How to earn points and stars" for the full rules.

How many puzzles are free?

Each puzzle type gives you 10 free puzzles on Easy and 5 on Medium and Hard. The number left shows beside each level. Unlock Everything gives unlimited puzzles at every level.

How do I reset my scores?

Open 🧩 Puzzles → 🏅 Best scores → Reset scores. Your daily chart of puzzles played is kept.

Purchase

What does Unlock Everything include?

A one-time purchase, not a subscription. It unlocks unlimited puzzles at every level, all six voice languages, all four text sizes, and removes ads. Reading numbers aloud and saying sums are free for everyone.

I reinstalled the app or got a new iPhone. How do I get my purchase back?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, then tap ☰ → Settings → Restore purchase (or Restore purchase on the Upgrade screen). It's free to restore.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose the BVCalculator purchase.

Other

How do I change the text size or theme?

Open ☰ → Settings. Choose Classic or Cartoon, and Small, Medium, Large or Extra large text (text size comes with Unlock Everything).

Where is my history?

Open ☰ → History. It keeps your last 50 sums, newest first. Tap one to keep calculating from its answer.

How do I get the latest version?

Update from the App Store. Small fixes also arrive by themselves: open ☰ → Settings → Check for updates, then restart the app.
