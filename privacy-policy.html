BVCalculator Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026

In short
  • No accounts, no sign-in, and we don't ask for your name, email or contacts.
  • Your sums, history, settings and puzzle scores stay on your device. We don't have a server that receives them.
  • The microphone is used only while you hold 🎤, to turn what you say into a sum.
  • The free version shows a banner ad from Google that asks for non-personalized ads only. BVCalculator does not track you.

This policy explains how the BVCalculator app for iPhone ("BVCalculator", "the app", "we") handles information. It applies to the app only.

1. Information that stays on your device

BVCalculator saves the following in the app's own storage on your device, so the app works the way you left it:

  • your calculation history (the last 50 sums);
  • settings such as theme, text size and voice language;
  • puzzle scores, stars, a daily history of puzzles played (kept for about a year), and recently shown puzzles so they don't repeat;
  • how many free puzzle tries you have used, and whether you have unlocked the full version.

This information is not sent to us. You can clear puzzle scores in the app (Best scores → Reset scores), and deleting the app deletes all of it.

2. Microphone and speech recognition

When you hold the 🎤 button, BVCalculator asks iOS for permission to use the microphone and speech recognition. While you hold the button, your voice is turned into text by Apple's speech recognition service, which may send the audio to Apple to process it. BVCalculator uses the text only to work out your sum or answer, and does not record, keep or share the audio or the text. Apple's handling is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

You can turn off microphone or speech recognition access at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security. The rest of the app keeps working.

3. Spoken numbers

Keys, answers and numbers are read aloud by the voices built into iOS, on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere to do this.

4. Ads (free version)

The free version shows a small banner ad from Google AdMob. BVCalculator asks Google for non-personalized ads only, limited to content rated PG and below, and never asks for permission to track you, so your device's advertising identifier is not available to the app or to Google.

To show an ad, measure it and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect information such as your IP address (used to estimate a general location), device and operating-system details, and how you interact with the ad. Google handles this information under its own policies: see how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy. The paid version shows no ads.

5. In-app purchase

The one-time "Unlock Everything" purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The app only learns whether the purchase is owned, so it can unlock the extras and restore them on your devices.

6. App updates

BVCalculator checks for small app updates from Expo, the service used to deliver them. These requests include technical details such as the app version, platform and an update identifier, and are handled under Expo's Privacy Policy. They do not include your sums, settings or scores.

8. What we don't do

  • We don't sell your information or share it with data brokers.
  • We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We don't use analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • We don't ask for your location, contacts, photos or camera.

9. Children

BVCalculator is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. The ads it requests are not personalized and are limited to content rated PG and below.

10. Your choices

  • Turn off microphone and speech recognition access in iOS Settings.
  • Reset puzzle scores in the app, or delete the app to remove everything it stored.
  • Unlock the full version to remove ads.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
